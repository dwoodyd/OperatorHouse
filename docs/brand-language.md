# Product Language Guide

**Operator House** is the product and workspace. **Specter** is the AI intelligence operator inside that workspace. An operator is always addressed by their name, or by the neutral fallback **Operator**—never as Specter.

Use **Operator House** for the application, workspace, product navigation, and public promise. Use **Specter** only for AI-authored messages, intelligence status, audits, briefings, and capability attribution. Use **AI operator** in headings when a first-time visitor benefits from an immediately understandable descriptor.

This distinction avoids asking a visitor to infer whether “Specter” is the product, the customer, or the system working on their behalf.
