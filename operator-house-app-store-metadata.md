### Full Description *(4,000 characters max)*

**Your consulting practice deserves a command center.**

Operator House is the AI-powered back office built for independent consultants, fractional executives, and creative agency operators who are done stitching together spreadsheets, Notion docs, and cold-email tools. Everything you need to find, qualify, close, and serve clients lives in one obsidian-dark, precision-built HQ.

**Six modules. One operator.**

**Lead Intelligence** — Paste a LinkedIn URL, email, or describe a prospect in plain language. Operator House runs a full Operator Audit: Vibe Check, Pain Points, Engineering Map, Legacy Play, and a Next Beat action. Every lead is scored 0–10 on intent and pushed to your pipeline in one tap.

**Client Pipeline** — A five-stage Kanban board (Discovery → Analysis → Strategy → Proposal → Closed) that tracks every deal, flags stale opportunities after seven days of inactivity, and surfaces your total pipeline value at a glance.

**Strategy Generator** — Choose from four output types — Full Strategy Doc, Quick Audit, Slide Deck Shell, or Outreach Email — and let the AI generate a grounded, client-specific document in seconds. Every strategy is saved to your history and downloadable as Markdown.

**The Vault** — Your personal knowledge base. Store frameworks, case studies, voice notes, templates, and research. The AI reads your Vault when generating strategies and auditing leads, so your proprietary methods are always in the room.

**Analytics** — Revenue trend charts, lead source breakdowns, weekly activity bars, and live metrics (total pipeline value, strategies built, active deals, hours saved). All derived from your real data.

**The Command Line** — A context-aware AI chat powered by your live pipeline and lead data. Ask it anything about your practice: "Which leads should I follow up with today?" or "Draft a follow-up email for Marcus Chen."

**Built for operators who move fast.**

Every screen loads with skeleton states so you never see a blank page. Every AI call has a 45-second timeout with a graceful retry. Rate limiting protects your account. Your data is scoped entirely to your account — no shared data, no cross-user leakage.

Operator House is not a CRM. It is not a project manager. It is the AI command center for the operator who runs their practice like a business.
